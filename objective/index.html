    The Objective

    Akashic Swaps isn't just a game you play — it's one you can build for. Here's the whole earning model, with nothing hidden.

    You build a bundle in the Editor. You publish it. Every player who buys it pays you. Your costs are small and fixed; your upside scales with how many people play.

    What it costs to start

    Subscription — first month$8.00
    Publish one bundle$2.00
    Your outlay$10.00

    What you keep per sale

    Bundle price (player pays)$2.00
    Your share — 90%$1.80
    Break-even~6 sales

    Clear about six copies and your first month is paid for. Everything after that is upside — and it keeps arriving as long as players keep buying.

    Now run the numbers yourself

    Let's be honest about this part. We can't promise you traffic — we're building this audience in public, and no Architect has earned a cent yet. But the model is simple enough that you can check it yourself. If the site draws some number of visitors a week, and a small share of them buy your $2 bundle, you keep $1.80 of each:

    Publish more than one bundle and each earns on its own. Your income is your reach times your catalog — there's no ceiling we impose on it.

    If the players come, the money goes to the people who make the levels.

    No guarantees, and no fine print. This is a wager on a growing audience, placed in the open — 90% to the Architect, 10% to keep the marketplace running. Not ads. Not timers. Not us skimming the top.
